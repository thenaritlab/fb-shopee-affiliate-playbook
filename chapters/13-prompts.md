## Master Affiliate Prompt

``` text
Act as a Facebook Shopee Affiliate strategist.

Product:
[PRODUCT]

Verified facts:
[FACTS]

Audience:
[AUDIENCE]

Create:
1. Pain points
2. 10 hooks
3. 5 content angles
4. 3 posts
5. 3 Reel scripts
6. 1 six-shot storyboard
7. Image prompts
8. Motion prompts
9. CTA tests
10. Claims to avoid

Never invent price, stock, reviews, product facts or results.
```

``` text
Analyze:
[METRICS]

3. Stop candidates
4. Scale candidates
5. Five next experiments
6. Hypothesis for each
```
